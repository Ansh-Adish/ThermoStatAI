Why raw temperature isn't enough

The downtown block reads 3°F cooler than the industrial zone, yet scores a higher thermal load — concrete and asphalt trap heat overnight and amplify the urban heat island by up to 10°F. A thermostat reading only air temperature gets this backwards. ThermoStat AI scores thermal load, not just temperature.
